Carlin-type gold prospectivity, Nevada

Two ways to read the same model. Favorable zone: the top k% of the area by model score, and how many known mines and how much gold it holds. Block probability: the calibrated frequency (isotonic regression) of blocks that contain at least one known mine. All figures come from spatial block cross-validation: each zone was scored by a model that never saw it.
